I'm Suman Tripuraneni, an infrastructure and platform engineer with more than 17 years of experience running large-scale systems in on-premises data centres and on AWS.

Most recently I've been a Senior Staff Engineer (Senior Architect) at Red Hat, working on platform architecture for large financial-services organizations. My work centres on Kubernetes and OpenShift, bare-metal and virtualized infrastructure, Terraform and Ansible automation, and observability with Prometheus and Grafana.

I also mentor engineers and write architecture decision records to help teams make durable platform choices. This blog is where I'll share what I learn while building and operating these systems.
